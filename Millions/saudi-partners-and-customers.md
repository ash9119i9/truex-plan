## 4. How to get in

**Events**

| Event | When / where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| LEAP | Riyadh. LEAP 2026 ran 31 Aug–3 Sep 2026; LEAP 2027 is 12–15 April 2027 | AWS and Microsoft made their Saudi region announcements here. Not always in February |
| FII (Future Investment Initiative) | Riyadh, every October | Investor and sovereign-fund audience |
| SAP NOW Riyadh | Riyadh | SAP ecosystem |

**Local setup**

- Government deals usually need a Saudi entity or a local prime contractor.
- Local-content rules favour local partners.
- Check the Ministry of Investment's regional-headquarters rules with a local lawyer before bidding.
