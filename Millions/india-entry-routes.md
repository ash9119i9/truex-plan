# Entry routes for a new Indian company

Partners come first. Big Saudi integrators like Solutions by stc and Elm rarely take a cold approach from a young foreign vendor, so use the routes below to earn a warm introduction to them. Checked on the web on 2026-10-20; confirm cohort timing and partner tiers with each organisation before applying.

## Where to start, in order

### 1. Arabian Accelerator (India Accelerator with Saudi Arabia's NTDP) — best fit

- Built for Indian AI startups expanding into Saudi Arabia: 6 months, a $15M fund, 8–10 startups per cohort.
- Covers procurement and compliance help, a booth at LEAP, introductions to curated companies, help drafting pilot proposals, and setting up a Saudi entity.
- Led about 45 Indian companies to LEAP 2026, where they met the Indian Ambassador.
- Cohort 1 applications ran Dec 2025 to Feb 2026 and are closed.
- Action: contact them now about cohort 2 — iaarabia.com.

### 2. Embassy of India, Riyadh (commercial wing) — free

- Its website has a registration link for Indian companies in Saudi Arabia and lists of Saudi business contacts.
- Action: ask the commercial representative for introductions and for any upcoming delegations.

### 3. Cloud and SAP partner programmes — join from India

| Programme | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Google Cloud ISV Startup Springboard | 12-week programme for AI startups that leads to co-selling with Google's sellers. Google's Saudi region is already live and hosts SAP BTP for regulated customers, so this one matters most |
| SAP PartnerEdge | Fits the SAP-agent niche; gives credibility with SAP Saudi Arabia and its Gold partners |
| Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program (ISV Success) | Azure Saudi Arabia East region due November 2026 |
| AWS Partner Network | AWS Saudi Arabia region due December 2026 |

### 4. Indian IT firms that already serve Saudi clients

TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCLTech, Tech Mahindra, LTIMindtree.

- They already have the Saudi client relationships and are easier for an Indian company to reach.
- They need AI and SAP-agent offerings to sell.
- Action: offer to be their white-label or co-sell agent platform.

### 5. LEAP 2027 (12–15 April 2027, Riyadh)

- Register interest for a Startup Pod now; a pod includes matchmaking with companies and government buyers.
- Cheaper option: Founder Pass (networking only).
- Go with the accelerator or a cloud partner, and book meetings before you arrive.
- Targets there: Solutions by stc, Elm, HUMAIN, SAP Saudi Arabia.

## Don't do yet

- **Don't open a Saudi entity before a pilot is signed.** A Ministry of Investment (MISA) entrepreneur licence allows 100% foreign ownership. You'll need one later for government deals; get a local adviser then.

## What partners will ask first

"Where does the data go?" Today it's AWS us-east-1 (AgentCore), which rules out regulated buyers. Before LEAP 2027, build:

1. A reference deployment that runs entirely in-country: frontend, platform, MongoDB, runtime container, model-gateway pointing at a self-hosted model.
2. A working SAP-agent demo.

These two things turn a partner meeting into a pilot.
