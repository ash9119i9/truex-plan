A5

Applied contract

Chart colour, without a rainbow.

The forms come from the chart system; the colour comes from chapter 02; the type comes from chapter 01. This page adds no values. These are the rules that kept it that way.

Contract · A5 / A5
01

Series stay neutral.

Use --cx-data and the ordered --cx-r1..r4 ramp. A rainbow is not a scale.

02

Signal is live or selected.

The now line, live dot and selected point may wear signal. A series never does.

03

Status annotates an exception.

A failed trace bar or an outlier carries a glyph and a word; it is not a category.

04

Identity never enters data.

Agent hues belong to agent surfaces, not bars, lines or scatter points.

05

Labels carry the reading.

Direct values, axes and the table underneath; colour is the last cue, never the only one.

06

Tokens only.

No raw hex in a chart. Dark and increased contexts are authored in color.css.
