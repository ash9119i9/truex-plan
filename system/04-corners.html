4.1

Geometry · curvature

A circle lands with a step.

Curvature is the second derivative of the edge. A circular arc holds one curvature and then stops. Your eye reads that stop as a faint notch. A superellipse starts at zero, peaks at 45°, and returns to zero — no step, no notch.

Kink · 4.1 / 7
Magnified 120px · same footprint

Same radius. Different derivative.

curvature continuity
OPTICSThe notch

A curvature step survives anti-aliasing. On a large surface it reads as a slight pinch exactly where the arc meets the line. The squircle removes the step by redistributing curvature: zero at the tangents, about 2.5× the circle's at 45°.

MATERIALThe stress point

A hard tangent concentrates stress in a physical fillet and breaks the run of a specular highlight. A continuous fillet spreads both.

TOUCHThe safe edge

The bounding box does not move. The continuous curve simply leaves more material in the corner and never presents a point to the hand.

MINDThe calm read

Contour is processed before detail. A continuous boundary removes the small alarm a sharp join creates, while the straight edges keep authority.

4.2

The family · K

A corner is a family, not a radius.

The parameter K chooses the curvature family. K 1 is a circle, K 2 a squircle, K ∞ approaches a square. The system fixes K at 2; move it here and the radius never changes — only the amount of material left in the corner.

4.4

Containment · concentric

A corner inside a corner.

An inner corner is not a smaller copy. It shares the corner center and returns the inset in radius. For a circle that offset is exact; for a squircle the center and both tangents align, and R − p is the closest same-exponent fit. Same radius offsets the center and opens the gap at 45°.

The rule
rinner = max(0,  Router − 16)

The corner center is the invariant. Padding moves the inner box, so the radius gives back the inset. For a circle that is exact; for a squircle it keeps the center and both tangents aligned.

The offset curve is not a superellipse. R − p is the closest same-exponent fit; the residual sits at 45° and is about a pixel at our 30px ceiling.

p 08→r 22 p 16→r 14 p 24→r 06 p 32→r 00

Same radius separates the centers; the gap opens at the diagonal and the inner corner reads as a separate, over-rounded object. Correct the radius, not the padding.

4.6

Charter · meaning

Some edges must stay round.

Continuous corners are for rectangles. A circle is a different claim: a person, a live process, a status, a capsule, a data mark. Those keep the circular arc — and the shape layer protects them by name.

Rules
  • ✓Use the radius ladder; never a raw number.
  • ✓Nest with r = R − p; never repeat R.
  • ✓Opt out circles, capsules and marks by class.
  • ✕Do not inflate the radius to fake softness.
  • ✕Do not use a mask for a corner the engine can draw.

The rectangle is calm. The circle is alive.

4.7

Contract · code

One property. One fallback.

The whole system is one shape token, one utility layer and one nesting rule. It loads after the page styles, declares no new colours and no new fonts, and leaves every footprint exactly where it was.

Contract · 4.7 / 7
corner.css 
/* truex · continuous corners · load last */
:root {
  --corner-surface: squircle; /* K 2 · |x|⁴ + |y|⁴ = 1 */
  --corner-mark: round;
  --corner-mode: var(--corner-surface);
}

@supports (corner-shape: squircle) {
  :where(html *) {
    corner-shape: var(--corner-mode);
  }
  .chip, .dot, .data-mark, .brand {
    corner-shape: round;
  }
}

.panel {
  border-radius: var(--r-lg);
  corner-shape: var(--corner-mode);
}

.panel__inner {
  border-radius: max(0px, calc(var(--r-lg) - var(--sp-4)));
}
