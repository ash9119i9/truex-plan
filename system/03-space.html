3.1

Space · scale · arithmetic

One lattice. No in-betweens.

Ten steps on a four-pixel base. The lower half builds controls; the upper half composes pages. A value between steps is not a refinement — it is a second system.

Proximity

Space groups before a line explains.

4

  • 4 — label to value, inside one row.
  • 8 — row to row, inside one group.
  • 24 — group to group, inside one block.
  • 48 — block to block, inside one section.

Rem-native by design. The scale grows with text size because every step is expressed in rem. At 200% the lattice is still the lattice; only the page gets longer.

01

Only these ten.

A new gap is a new token request, not a local decision. Raw px never ships in layout.

02

Two lanes, one scale.

Control spacing lives inside a component. Composition spacing lives between components. Never swap the lanes.

03

Space groups; lines separate.

Reach for the next step before reaching for a border. One divider per group, maximum.

04

Rhythm is vertical, too.

Margins and gaps come from the same ten steps. A horizontal system with arbitrary vertical air is not a system.

3.3

Form · corner · superellipse

Five surfaces. One continuous curve.

A corner is not a quarter-circle. Five rectangular surfaces share one continuous geometry — a superellipse. The shape travels through the border, the shadow and the focus halo; the 3px data mark and the capsule stay round on purpose.

Corner · 03 / 08
Corner character · magnified 44px

Same radius. Different curve.

Native squircle · Chromium

44px is a magnified study. In product the ladder runs 6–30; the difference is legible first on 22–30.

Math A superellipse

squircle is superellipse(2); round is superellipse(1). One curve replaces the arc and spreads the change in curvature across the corner.

Physics Stress and light

A hard curvature change concentrates stress in a material and breaks the run of a specular highlight. A continuous fillet spreads both.

Psychology Contour bias

The eye is a contour detector. Curved boundaries are processed faster and, on average, rated more pleasant; the rectangle keeps order, the curve removes threat.

Human nature Calm authority

Sharp corners raise alertness. Capsules read as friendly, circles as human. The squircle sits between: precise, but never tense.

3.4

Form · light · optics

Three lifts. One light.

Shadow is a response to overlap. If a surface does not float, it does not cast. One light above and slightly front; hairline borders carry everything that stays in flow.

01

If it doesn't overlap, it doesn't cast.

In-flow panels use a hairline and a tonal surface. Shadow is reserved for layers that actually sit above the page.

02

Border and shadow travel together.

A shadow never replaces a border. The hairline holds the edge; the shadow describes the distance.

03

Two raised levels, maximum.

A card inside a panel inside a dialog is a box in a box. The third level becomes a well or a line.

04

Only the signal may glow.

No coloured shadows. Focus, live state and the primary action are the only elements allowed to cast in blue.

3.5

Layout · grid · divisibility

Twelve columns. Three states.

Twelve divides by 2, 3, 4 and 6, so the grid can split without fractions. Wide uses twelve columns with 24px gaps; medium eight with 16; compact four with 12. The grid steps with the text size, and components must survive the spaces in between.

3.6

Layout · score · chunking

Space is the score.

A page reads as stanzas, not a list of cards. Sections breathe at 64–96, blocks at 32–64, groups at 24–32, items at 8–12. The eye chunks by proximity; the spacing should make the next thought wait.

Rhythm · 06 / 08
Chapter head

A page is a sequence of stanzas.

Headings sit eight above their copy. Copy sits twenty-four above the next block. Blocks sit forty-eight apart; sections ninety-six. The spacing does the work a divider would otherwise pretend to do.

01

One idea per stanza.

If a section needs two focal points, it is two sections. Split the thought before shrinking the air.

02

Section gaps are not component gaps.

Sixty-four to ninety-six between sections; twenty-four to thirty-two inside a group. Mixing the two flattens the page.

03

Use gap, not margins.

Gaps never collapse and never surprise. Margins are reserved for text defaults owned by type.css.

04

Respect the fold as a stanza.

The first viewport carries one complete thought: stage, claim and a reason to continue. Not three half-thoughts.

3.7

Aesthetics · contract · fluency

Eight rules. A clean instrument.

The aesthetic is not a finish applied at the end. It is the result of eight structural decisions made before the first component: what groups, what separates, what lifts and what stays quiet.

01

Hairline first.

Structure with one-pixel lines and tonal surfaces. Shadow only appears when a surface leaves the page.

02

Space groups, lines separate.

Proximity is the primary divider. A border is the fallback when space cannot do the job.

03

One focal point per screen.

One claim, one live object, one action. Everything else is chassis and should read as chassis.

04

Shadow means overlap.

If two surfaces are not layered, neither casts. Elevation is a fact about the interface, not a mood.

05

No boxes inside boxes.

Two levels of container, maximum. The third becomes a well, a line or nothing at all.

06

Density follows function.

Instruments tighten; reading opens. Density is decided by the work the zone does, not by the viewport.

07

Optical beats mathematical.

A four-pixel icon gap and a five-pixel pill inset can be correct. The lattice guides; the eye signs off.

08

Nothing decorates without meaning.

A dot is live. A hatch is estimated. A grid is a canvas. If a mark carries no state, it is noise.

3.8

Build · the file

One file. No raw pixels.

The chapter ships as space.css: the lattice, the continuous corner, the three lifts and the shell metrics. Product code consumes tokens; it never authors a new gap, corner or shadow.

Build · 08 / 08

Spacing is a token, not a judgement. If a layout needs a value between steps, the composition is wrong. Reach for a different step or a different structure.

Elevation lives with space. Shadows moved out of color.css because they describe distance, not hue. The dark overrides sit in the same file; components only name the tier.

The shell is measured. Rail, thread, inspector and overlay are tokens, not numbers repeated in three stylesheets. The page grid follows the text size.

Shape is a variable. Radius sets size; --corner-surface sets character. Chromium renders corner-shape natively; Safari and Firefox keep the round fallback without changing layout.
